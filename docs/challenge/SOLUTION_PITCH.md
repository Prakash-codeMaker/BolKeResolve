# BolKeResolve — Voice-to-Proof Consumer Resolution Workspace

**Great Indian AI Internship Challenge 2026** · **Primary fit:** Real Deal  
**Tagline:** *Speak the issue. Keep the proof. Know the next step.*

## The problem

A failed UPI payment, pending transfer or delayed refund can leave someone juggling screenshots, reference numbers and support messages. A merchant may say a refund has been initiated, but that statement does not prove the money was credited. The user needs a clearer next step—and a reliable way to keep track of what was said and what evidence exists.

## The solution

**BolKeResolve turns a spoken or typed consumer complaint into a user-reviewed, evidence-aware case record.** It brings the user's story, supporting files, practical guidance, support commitments and follow-up history into one workspace.

## How it works

1. **Speak or type:** Explain the issue naturally in Hindi, Hinglish or English.
2. **Review:** Inspect and correct the transcript and important case details. Unknown information stays unknown rather than being guessed.
3. **Organise:** Keep receipts, screenshots, support replies and case events together with their source and provenance.
4. **Track commitments:** Record the original support wording, amount, conditions and stated timeframe.
5. **Follow through:** Add later replies or user-reported updates as separate events, then export a portable Resolution Passport.

## What makes it different: Promise-to-Proof

A support message saying “your ₹850 refund has been initiated” is not proof that ₹850 reached the user's account. BolKeResolve preserves the original promise and records later updates separately, so the history does not silently turn an assurance into a verified outcome.

## Gnani AI integration

BolKeResolve integrates **Gnani AI Prisma** for speech-to-text and **Gnani AI Timbre** for text-to-speech. The speech services power the voice experience; the case workflow, evidence relationships, deterministic guidance and Promise-to-Proof ledger are application logic.

## Trust boundaries

BolKeResolve helps users organise information and understand possible next steps. It does **not** access bank accounts, independently verify UPI transactions, contact merchants, execute payments or refunds, or automatically file complaints. User-reported information remains labelled as user-reported. Demonstration case details are synthetic.
