What the service does

BolKeResolve helps people organise a story about a payment or consumer issue, review possible facts, prepare a source-based checklist and support-message draft, and track updates they choose to record. It does not check bank, UPI, merchant or government systems and does not submit a complaint or payment instruction on a user's behalf.

Information that may be processed

  • The story, messages, language preference and facts you choose to provide, such as merchant, amount, date and issue status.
  • Case notes, user-reported progress events, follow-up dates and support-message drafts.
  • Evidence files you intentionally upload on the website. Do not upload unredacted statements or unrelated personal information.
  • If you use WhatsApp, your WhatsApp sender number is encrypted at rest in the application database and a keyed fingerprint is used for lookups. Meta also processes WhatsApp messages under its own terms and privacy practices.
  • If you send a voice note and transcription is enabled, the audio is sent to Gnani's speech-to-text service to produce a transcript. Confirm the provider terms, applicable data-processing agreement, data location and retention before launch.
  • Technical information needed to run and secure the service, such as request times, delivery receipts and error types. The application should not log access tokens, raw phone numbers or complete message payloads.

Purposes and choices

Information is used to prepare the case workflow you request, save and display your case, associate an opted-in WhatsApp contact with a case, transcribe voice notes when requested, and send follow-up reminders only where the reminder feature is configured and you have opted in. WhatsApp opt-in is recorded when you send START. Send STOP to stop future WhatsApp messages; STOP does not automatically delete a previously saved case.

Before public launch, the operator must identify and document the appropriate legal basis for each processing purpose and ensure that consent notices are specific, informed, easy to understand and as easy to withdraw as to give where applicable.

Service providers and locations

Depending on enabled features, information may be processed by Meta/WhatsApp for messaging, by Gnani for speech transcription, and by the infrastructure/database host selected by the operator. The actual hosting region, international transfers, provider retention and contractual terms must be confirmed and disclosed by the operator. Do not describe the service as India-only or claim a provider never retains data unless a current contract/documentation supports it.

Security and sensitive information

We use safeguards including server-side credentials, webhook signature verification, case access checks in production mode, and encrypted storage of WhatsApp phone numbers. No technical safeguard is perfect, and sensitive-text detection may miss content. Never send OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords, full card/account numbers, remote-access codes, Aadhaar/PAN numbers or other credentials. For suspected unauthorised transactions, contact your bank/payment provider immediately through its official channel.
